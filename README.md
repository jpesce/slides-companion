Free presenter tools for Google Slides™, in an extension for Chrome-based browsers. It works on top of Slides' own slideshow, so your videos, animations and clicker keep working. Get it from the [Chrome Web Store](https://chromewebstore.google.com/detail/image-spotlight-for-googl/habmkngfcidcchgmdpampgcbmnllkbhd).

https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/ec264cc1-df3a-48b8-a82a-06445b620273

## Features

- 🔍 **Spotlight:** see any picture full screen, while presenting or editing.
- 🔗 **Links:** open links over your slide, and press <kbd>Esc</kbd> to come back.
- 🔎 **Zoom:** zoom into a detail with your trackpad or mouse, and scrolling never skips a slide.
- 🖊️ **Pen:** what you draw stays on the slide for next time.
- ⏱️ **Timer:** count down in presenter view, so you finish on time.
- 📝 **Floating notes:** your speaker notes in a small window over the slideshow, left out when you share only the slideshow.
- ✏️ **Live edits:** changes to the deck reach a slideshow that's already running.
- 🖼️ **Download image:** save any picture as it was added to the deck.

🕊️ Free and open source, with no account and no servers: everything happens in your browser.

Each feature has its own page in Settings, with what it does and its choices: click the extension's button at the top of Slides' side toolbar, whose eyes follow your mouse.

Looking for something it doesn't do, like a phone remote or audience polls? [How Slides Companion compares](BENCHMARK.md) sets it side by side with Google Slides' own tools and other projects.

## Getting started

1. Install from the [Chrome Web Store](https://chromewebstore.google.com/detail/image-spotlight-for-googl/habmkngfcidcchgmdpampgcbmnllkbhd).
2. Open any Google Slides presentation.
3. Present as usual: the tools are there when you need them.

### Manual installation

> [!NOTE]
> This method is recommended only for developers and advanced users.<br/>
> For the best experience, install the extension from the [Chrome Web Store](https://chromewebstore.google.com/detail/image-spotlight-for-googl/habmkngfcidcchgmdpampgcbmnllkbhd).

Download a [release](https://github.com/jpesce/slides-companion-for-google-slides/releases), unzip it, and [load it as an unpacked extension](https://developer.chrome.com/docs/extensions/get-started/tutorial/hello-world#load-unpacked).
