# The README's tone: emoji and playful lines

**Problem:** every feature bullet in README.md starts with an emoji (🔍 🔗 🔎 🖊️ ⏱️ 📝 ✏️ 🖼️ ⌨️ 🌍 🕊️), and a few lines are playful: the extension's button "whose eyes follow your mouse", "speaks Slides' language". Some readers take that as warmth. Others take it as the template look of generated READMEs, which works against the "looks like Google built it" feel the rest of the project goes for.

**Evidence of demand:** the docs review of 9 November 2026, asked for by the maintainer ("no AI slop?"), flagged them as the main tells left. Card 330 fixed the facts and left the tone to decide here.

**Approach:** the maintainer picks one:
- Keep both. They're the README's voice, and Image Spotlight's README had emoji too.
- Drop the emoji and keep the plain lines, the way Google's own help pages read.
- Drop both.

Then apply it to README.md. STORE.md uses neither, and stays as it is.

**Done when:**
- [ ] The maintainer has chosen, and README.md follows it.
